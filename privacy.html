Privacy Policy

Restored: Art Puzzles
SupportPrivacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Restored is a puzzle game made by a solo developer (contact: support requests).

Restored does not collect, store or share any personal information.

  • No accounts, sign-in, advertising, analytics or tracking.
  • Your progress, settings and streaks are saved only on your own device. On iPhone and iPad they may be synced through your own iCloud account by Apple; the developer never sees them.
  • The optional daily reminder is a notification scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • If Game Center is turned on in a future version, achievements are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.
  • The "View at The Met" links open The Metropolitan Museum of Art's website in your browser, which has its own privacy policy.
  • The web version stores progress in your browser's local storage only.

Children: Restored is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
